Captions for the per-split supplementary tables.
Per-region summary tables (no subject-level rows). Each table is
reissued on the cross-validated training split and on the held-out
test split. n is the number of subjects contributing to the split.

[SuppTable02_TLE_asymmetrycorr_DKT_crossvalidated.csv]
Across-subject regional concordance in the epilepsy dataset using the Desikan-Killiany-Tourville (DKT) atlas. For each brain region, Spearman correlation and mean bias of FlowGAN-synthetic and ASL asymmetry index are computed against real FDG-PET across subjects; Corr_Diff is the FlowGAN minus ASL difference in Spearman r (positive favours FlowGAN). Computed on the cross-validated training split (n = 58 subjects).

[SuppTable06_TLE_congruency_DKT_crossvalidated.csv]

Higher values indicate stronger agreement. Computed on the cross-validated training split (n = 58 subjects).
